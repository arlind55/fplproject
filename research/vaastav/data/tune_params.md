## What was shipped

The coordinate descent also lowered goal_cal (DEF/GKP 0.85 -> 0.75, MID/FWD about 9% lower) and assist_cal
(1.38 -> 1.30). Those were **not** adopted: projected goal points were already below actual goal points for
defenders, and lowering them only helps squared error by shrinking noisy projections, not by being right;
FPL also changed the assist rules in 2025/26, so older seasons pull assist_cal the wrong way. cs_cal = 1.1 was
kept because it lowers both the error and the defender/keeper over-projection.

Shipped: k_min 0.25, decay 0.5, n_recent 10, lam 0.25, k_xa 2, k_saves 16, k_bonus 12, k_cards 40, cs_cal 1.1,
team model HALF_LIFE 10 and PRIOR_GAMES 1.
