## lede
GW9's deadline is Saturday at 11:00, not the usual Friday evening. Two things shape the week. João Pedro is out and in two-thirds of squads, and Bournemouth have the easiest fixture run in the league just as their results catch up with their numbers. This week's feature posts cover both in depth: <a href="./gw9-joao-pedro-replacements.html">who replaces João Pedro</a> and <a href="./gw9-team-form.html">which teams are really in form</a>. ## scorecard
One hit from three. <strong>Fernandes</strong> didn't play at all, so the captaincy call scored 0. That's the second time in three weeks the model's captain has blanked, and it's the reason the model now gives him only a 71% chance of starting. <strong>Tavernier</strong> scored at Old Trafford in Bournemouth's 3-0 win for 10 points. <strong>Thomas</strong> of Coventry managed 1 point in 59 minutes and now carries a 75% flag.

## captain
This is the easiest captaincy call of the season so far. <strong>Haaland</strong> projects at 6.5, more than a point clear of <strong>Gabriel</strong> (5.3), and he's at home to a Brighton side the model concedes 1.92 expected goals to. His 1-in-10 ceiling is 11 points before doubling, and he blanks (2 points or fewer) in only 3% of simulations. With 78% ownership, not captaining him is a big risk for little reason.

If you don't own him, <strong>Tavernier</strong> (5.0 at home to Leeds) and <strong>Palmer</strong> (4.9 at home to United, on penalties) are the alternatives. <strong>Gabriel</strong> is the interesting one. Arsenal have the best clean-sheet odds of the week (36%) even away at Anfield and he has a 9-point ceiling.

## fixtures
City at home to Brighton and Bournemouth at home to Leeds are the two attacking fixtures, at 1.92 and 1.83 model xG. Brentford v Forest (1.74) is third, which is part of the Thiago case. Arsenal's 36% clean-sheet chance at Liverpool is the only one above 31%, and the model rates them the stronger side at Anfield (1.34 xG to 1.01). Coventry v Sunderland is the lowest-scoring game on the model, and Coventry, Sunderland and Spurs all have clean-sheet odds of 30% or better, which makes them good spots for cheap defenders.

Expected GW9 total with Haaland captained: 58.5.

## crowd
The crowd and the model agree on almost everything this week. Tavernier, Jackson, Isak, Konsa and Evanilson are all being bought and all rank in the model's top eight at their position. The exception is <strong>Fernandes</strong>. 179,000 managers are selling him after his GW8 absence, but he's still the model's sixth-best midfielder over five weeks, even at a 71% start chance. If he's in United's XI on Saturday, holding is fine. Nobody should be buying Calafiori (75% flag) or Saka (out).
