João Pedro is out: who replaces him

João Pedro missed GW8's 2-0 home defeat to Spurs, and FPL now has him flagged out with a thigh injury and no return date. He's in 66% of squads, so this is the biggest transfer decision of the week. Here's what the projections say about replacing him, and whether you need to.

Sell or hold?

The model has him at 6.1 points over GW9–13. That's what's left after it allows for a gradual return from GW10 onwards. Fit, he would project at 23.3, which would make him the best forward in the game after Haaland. So the question isn't whether he's good. It's whether he's worth carrying for two or three weeks.

The calendar helps a little. After GW10 there's an international break, and GW11 isn't until 21 November, four weeks after he was first flagged. If the injury is a short one, he could be back for GW11. Even then, selling wins on the model's numbers. Swap him for Thiago and you gain about 8.6 points across GW9 and GW10. If João Pedro then returns fully fit for GW11, you give back about 1.7 over GW11–13. That's roughly +7 points over five weeks for one free transfer, and more if his return slips.

If you have no free transfer, benching him for two weeks is reasonable, as long as your other two forwards are playing. FPL squads need three forwards, so the replacement has to be a forward. You can't use his slot for a midfielder.

Isak has the best underlying numbers on the list (0.52 xG per 90) but the lowest start chance (76%). He's also the only one who costs more than João Pedro. Arsenal at home in GW9 is the hardest fixture any of these players face this week. He's a better buy for GW11 than for GW9.

What about Chelsea?

Chelsea have played twice without João Pedro this season. They lost 3-0 at Brentford and 2-0 at home to Spurs, and didn't score in either. Their fixtures are good (Man United, Leeds and Palace at home in the next five), but the backup forwards don't make the model's list. Welbeck projects 8.5 points over the five weeks with a 19% start chance, and the model gives Emegha a 2% start chance. If you want Chelsea attacking exposure, it comes through Palmer (24.6 over five weeks, on penalties) or Rogers (20.3), not through his stand-in.

Chelsea have also scored 17 goals from 10.5 expected, the biggest overperformance in the league. Without their leading scorer that is more likely to shrink than grow, so we wouldn't pay extra for Palmer to "replace" João Pedro's goals.

The call. Sell João Pedro if you have a free transfer. Thiago is the straight swap at the same price. Calvert-Lewin or Barry if you want £1.7–2.2m back for an upgrade elsewhere. Bench him for two weeks if you'd need a hit. He's worth bringing back once he's training, because fit he's the second-best forward in the model.
