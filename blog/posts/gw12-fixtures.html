It picks out the teams to target and the teams to avoid, and the best games in GW12. For which players to buy from these teams, see who to target.

The GW12 games to target

Brighton at home to Newcastle is the best attacking fixture of the week. The model expects 2.13 goals, more than anyone else. Groß (5.5) and De Cuyper (4.9) are two of the week's top-projected players.

Leeds at home to Coventry has the best clean-sheet odds of the week, at 42%. Arsenal at home to City is second at 36%, which says a lot about the Arsenal defence.

Man City at Arsenal is the week's big game, and the model expects it to be tight (1.50 to 1.03 in Arsenal's favour). That's why Haaland isn't the model's GW12 captain. The targets post has the captaincy call.

The one-line version. Arsenal, Liverpool, City and Bournemouth have the best six-game runs, so own their players. Villa and Forest get the biggest improvement. Avoid Hull, Ipswich, Everton and Palace. In GW12, the games to target are Brighton at home to Newcastle and Leeds at home to Coventry.
